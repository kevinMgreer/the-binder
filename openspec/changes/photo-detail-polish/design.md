## Context

See proposal.md for why. Read on branch `agent/form-sort-polish` (PR #36, not merged yet):

- `src/app/PhotoSlot.tsx` renders, per side, a `.photo-slot` row: a `Frame` (96 dp via `.photo-slot .frame`) and an `.actions` pair, "Scan front" / "Retake front" and "Pick front from photos". `take()` calls `acquirePhoto(photoSource, side, how)` (scanner with system-camera fallback), shows "Processing photo…", reports busy through `onBusy` so `CardForm` disables Save, and shows a permission or load error as `role="alert"`. There is no way to remove a photo today: `Photos` in `src/ports/index.ts` is `Partial<Record<CardSide, CapturedPhoto>>`, and `saveCard` in `src/usecases/cards.ts` only replaces a side (queueing the old `fileId` / `thumbId` with `queueDelete`).
- `src/sync/engine.ts` `uploadPhotos` uploads every pending local photo and writes its Drive ids into the card's side, whatever that side holds now.
- `src/app/CardDetail.tsx`: `.title-row` (h2 plus serial `.tag`), `.title-rule`, `p.subtitle` "year set · #number", `dl.facts` (Manufacturer, Subset, Insert, Team, Parallel, Condition, Quantity, Traits), notes, `.photo-row` of `PhotoThumb` (thumbnail via `useThumbUrl`, `.photo-thumb` 140 dp), last-edited line, `.actions` with Edit, then `.danger-zone` with Delete and an inline `role="alertdialog"` (Confirm delete / Keep) rendered at the very end of the screen.
- `src/app/app.css`: `.title-row` has `min-height: 56px` and `align-items: center`, made for the top-level screens where it also holds the gear. On the detail screen a one-line title therefore floats in a 56 dp band; then the rule, then the subtitle with `--space-2` above and `--space-5` below, then the facts' own top border. The subtitle sits alone between two rules, which is the "odd placement" the owner sees. (Read from the CSS, not from a screenshot; group 2 checks it in the e2e design harness.)
- List: `src/app/CardList.tsx` `ROW_HEIGHT = 100`, `.thumb-slot` 67 by 67, thumbnails 320 px long edge (`THUMB_LONG_EDGE` in `src/photos/compress.ts`; full photos 1600 px).
- Form: `.actions` (Cancel then Save, from form-sort-polish) sits after the save-blocked `role="alert"` at the end of the long form. The page itself scrolls (no inner scroller). form-sort-polish added `body::after`, a fixed strip of `--inset-bottom` in the ground colour at `z-index: 0`, and `--inset-top` / `--inset-bottom` variables.
- Android back: `App.tsx` `goBack` calls the newest entry of `openSheets` (`src/app/Sheet.tsx`) before it changes screens. `Sheet` pushes its `onClose`; `CardForm` pushes its discard handler the same way (form-sort-polish). `Sheet` has a `compact` flag (form-sort-polish) for content-height sheets.

**Archive order.** form-sort-polish is still active and modifies `card-photos` "Photos are taken from the card form". This change modifies the same requirement, so its MODIFIED block is written against the text as form-sort-polish leaves it (Cancel then Discard), and **this change archives after form-sort-polish**. No other requirement here is touched by form-sort-polish: its `app-shell`, `app-theme` ("Every screen wears the card-back look"), `card-form-guidance` (two ADDED requirements) and `collection-list` ("The user chooses the list order") deltas are left alone. The sticky bar is a new ADDED requirement rather than a MODIFIED "Cancel sits left of Save", which does not exist in the main specs until form-sort-polish archives.

## Decisions

- **Tile plus a compact `Sheet` as the chooser.** `PhotoSlot` becomes one tile: a `button.photo-tile` holding the existing `Frame` (96 dp) and, while the side is empty, a camera icon and the word "Front" / "Back" over the placeholder. Its accessible name is "Add front photo" when empty and "Change front photo" when filled (the visible word is part of the name). "Filled" keeps today's rule for Retake: a captured photo or a stored one (`card.photos[side]`), even before the thumbnail loads. Tapping opens `<Sheet compact label="Front photo">` with the side's actions as full-width buttons. `Sheet` already gives a scrim, Escape, a Close button, focus on open and the `openSheets` back handling, so the chooser's Cancel is the sheet's Close. `CardForm` renders the two tiles inside one `.photo-tiles` flex row (`gap: var(--space-3)`); per-side "Processing photo…" and error messages render below that row.
  Alternatives: an anchored popup menu (needs positioning, outside-tap and focus-trap code, and there is no house menu component); keeping the two buttons per side but in two columns (still two rows of buttons, the owner asked for tiles); a long-press on a filled tile for Remove (hidden gesture). $0: markup and CSS only.

- **Action names keep today's accessible names.** The chooser buttons read "Scan front", "Pick front from photos", "Retake front", and the new "Remove front photo". Visible text equals the accessible name, so TalkBack, the label-in-name rule and the existing tests' names all hold, and the existing scenarios keep their wording. The owner's shorter wording ("Scan", "Pick from photos", "Pick another") would need `aria-label`s that do not contain the visible text, or tests renamed for no user gain inside a sheet already titled "Front photo"; see Open Questions. A filled side keeps "Pick front from photos" rather than "Pick another".
  Alternatives: short visible labels with longer `aria-label`s (breaks label-in-name for "Pick from photos"); short labels as names (every test and scenario renamed). $0: none.

- **Remove is `null` in `Photos`.** `Photos` becomes `Partial<Record<CardSide, CapturedPhoto | null>>`: `undefined` means untouched, `null` means "remove the stored photo". Remove sets `null` only when the card has a stored photo on that side, else `undefined` (a removed capture on the add form is simply gone, so the form is not dirty because of it). `saveCard` queues the old `fileId` / `thumbId` for both a replacement and a removal and writes `photos[side] = null` for a removal. `uploadPhotos` in the engine skips (and marks uploaded) a pending photo whose card side is now `null`, so a photo removed before it uploaded never reaches Drive. In `CardForm`, `dirty` counts `photos[side] !== undefined`, Identify and the "Identify with Claude" visibility ignore a side set to `null`, and `PhotoSlot` shows no stored thumbnail for it. Edit-then-Cancel discards the `null` with the rest of the form state, so the stored photo stays.
  Alternatives: a new `PhotoStore.remove(cardId, side)` port method that also deletes the local blobs (three adapters plus the fake for a few KB of thumbnail; local full blobs are already LRU-evicted, local thumbs are cleared on sign-out; left as a `shortcut:` comment in `saveCard`); removing immediately on tap instead of on Save (Cancel could no longer undo it). $0: Drive deletes use the existing trash queue.

- **List: 96 dp slot, 112 dp rows, thumbnails unchanged.** `.thumb-slot` becomes 96 by 96 and `ROW_HEIGHT` 112 (96 slot, 8 dp above and below inside the button, the li's 8 dp bottom padding keeps the gap to the rule). A 96 dp slot on a phone of density 2.6 to 3 needs about 250 to 290 px, so the stored 320 px thumbnails stay sharp. The comment above `ROW_HEIGHT` is updated. About 4.7 rows fit on a 360 by 800 screen instead of 5.3; the "at least three full card rows" first-screen rule (`collection-list`) still holds by the estimate and is re-checked by its existing e2e test.
  Alternatives: keep 100 dp rows with a 92 dp slot (the thumbnail touches the rules); 480 px thumbnails (only new photos benefit, existing ones keep 320 px since nothing is re-generated, and every list row decodes a larger image). $0: none.

- **Detail photos: 200 dp, full photo when it is local.** `.photo-thumb` becomes 200 dp tall; a landscape frame is capped at half the row (`max-width` as today). `useThumbUrl(cardId, side, enabled, version, kind = "thumb")` gains a `kind` argument that reads `photos.getFull` instead of `photos.getThumb`; `PhotoThumb` calls it twice and shows `full ?? thumb`, so the thumbnail appears at once and the full photo replaces it when the local read returns. The detail screen never downloads: the full photo arrives locally after the first save on this device or after the zoom view (`PhotoView`) has fetched and cached it. The 1600 px full photo at 200 dp is fine: it is the image `PhotoView` already shows, about 400 KB, decoded once per side while the screen is open. `THUMB_LONG_EDGE` stays 320 px; a thumbnail stretched to 200 dp (about 550 px) is soft but only shows until the full photo is local. `Frame` keeps its orientation cache key, so swapping thumb for full does not flip the frame.
  Alternatives: always the thumbnail (blurry at 200 dp); download the full photo when the detail opens (mobile data and offline cost, and the zoom view already does it on demand); 480 px thumbnails (see above). $0: no network calls added.

- **Delete left of Edit; centred dialog inline in `CardDetail`.** The action row is the existing `.actions` flex row with `.detail-actions`: Delete first (`button.danger.outline`: transparent, danger text and danger border) then Edit (`button.primary`), both `flex: 1` so they share the width, and both are real buttons with the base button's 48 dp height, padding and frame radius. Cancel left, Save right is the same pattern. Colour keeps Delete distinct from the ink-filled Edit, with the 8 dp row gap between them. `.danger-zone` leaves the markup of the detail screen (its CSS stays for Settings' Sign out). The dialog renders inside a `div.backdrop.center` (the existing scrim and `z-index: 10`, plus `align-items: center; justify-content: center` and the gutter and both insets as padding): `role="alertdialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-label="Delete card"`, the question, then a row with Keep (`autoFocus`) and Delete (`button.danger`). While it is open, `CardDetail` pushes `keep` onto `openSheets` exactly like `CardForm`'s discard handler, so Android back is Keep; the scrim's `onClick` and an Escape `onKeyDown` call `keep` too, and `keep` returns focus to the screen's Delete button (a ref). Delete in the dialog calls the existing `onDelete`, so soft delete and the photo trash queue are unchanged. The `role="alertdialog"` rule in `app.css` keeps the panel look; the dialog gets `margin: 0` and a `max-width` of 320 px.
  Alternatives: reuse `Sheet` (a bottom sheet with a Close head, not a centred question); a shared `ConfirmDialog` component also used by the form's Discard prompt (no second caller in this change; form-sort-polish's inline prompt stays as it is, see Open Questions); `window.confirm` (unstyled, blocks the WebView). $0: none.

- **Detail header block.** Wrap title row, quiet line and a new secondary line in `header.detail-head` (flex column, `gap: var(--space-1)`, no margins on its children), with the `.title-rule` after it. Inside it `.title-row` drops the 56 dp minimum and aligns to the first baseline, so a one- or two-line name sits flush at the top and the stamp stays on the first line. The secondary line is `[card.team, card.parallel?.name].filter(Boolean).join(" · ")`, rendered only when non-empty, in the same muted label colour as the quiet line. Team and Parallel leave `facts`. The facts' top border is dropped when they follow the title rule directly (no two rules in a row). Assumptions: the owner's "odd vertical alignment" is the 56 dp band plus the subtitle floating between two rules; team and parallel are better as header text than as fact rows; the stamp stays at the right end of the title row as today.
  Alternatives: keep Team and Parallel in the facts too (the same value twice on one screen); put the rule directly under the title as on other screens and the lines below it (that is today's layout, the one the owner dislikes). $0: CSS and markup only.

- **Sticky bottom bar for Cancel / Save.** This is standard practice: Material's bottom app bar and full-screen form patterns pin the confirming action at the bottom where the thumb is. `CardForm` wraps the two alerts (`errors.card`, save-blocked) and `.actions` in `div.form-bar`, the last child of the form, after the dialogs, so a dialog at the form's end scrolls fully above the bar. CSS: `position: sticky; bottom: var(--inset-bottom); z-index: 1;` panel background, `border-top: var(--rule) solid var(--color-rule)`, `padding: var(--space-3) var(--gutter)` with negative side margins of `var(--gutter)` so it spans the screen. Sticky (not fixed) means the bar returns to its place in the flow at the end of the form, so the last field always clears it with no extra padding, and it stays above form-sort-polish's `body::after` strip, which keeps painting the inset under it. `html { scroll-padding-bottom: calc(var(--target-min) + 2 * var(--space-3) + var(--inset-bottom)); }` keeps a field brought into view by focus (the blocked-save focus, tab order) clear of the bar. The duplicate dialog's first button gets `autoFocus`, because Save can now be tapped at the top of the form and the dialog renders at the form's end. The bar stays visible while the keyboard is open: there is no keyboard plugin, `AndroidManifest.xml` sets no `windowSoftInputMode`, and a `visualViewport` guess is fragile; the phone check decides whether this needs a follow-up.
  Alternatives: Save in a header at the top right (far from the thumb, splits Cancel from Save, and the blocked message would sit far from the fields' end); a floating action button (covers content, Material reserves it for a screen's main creative action, and it has no place for Cancel or the message); `position: fixed` with bottom padding on the form (needs the bar height hard-coded twice). $0: CSS and markup only.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The sticky bar covers about 72 dp of a 360 by 800 screen, more with the keyboard open] → the form scrolls under it and focus keeps fields clear via `scroll-padding-bottom`; the phone check covers typing in Notes with the keyboard open.
- [Bigger list rows show fewer cards per screen] → about 4.7 instead of 5.3; the first-screen e2e test keeps the three-row minimum.
- [200 dp detail photos push Edit and Delete down] → Team and Parallel leave the fact list, and the e2e check asserts both actions in view for a card with two photos and four facts. A card with many facts and notes scrolls to its actions, which the scenario allows.
- [A removed photo leaves its local blobs until sign-out or eviction] → invisible (every reader is gated on `card.photos[side]`), a few KB per thumbnail; `shortcut:` comment names the upgrade (a `PhotoStore.remove`).
- [Spec wording churn] → "Destructive actions stay apart" is replaced by "Destructive actions are distinct" (REMOVED plus ADDED; Delete is a danger-outlined button, no longer text only), because a scenario cannot be dropped from a MODIFIED block.
- [Archive order] → archive form-sort-polish first; archiving this change first would apply its "Photos are taken from the card form" text and then form-sort-polish's would overwrite it with the older two-button wording.

## Migration Plan

No data migration: stored photos, thumbnails and cards are read as they are. Rebase onto `main` once PR #36 merges, then archive form-sort-polish before this change. Rollback is a revert of the branch.
